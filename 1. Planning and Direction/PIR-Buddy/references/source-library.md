## External references (from the folder README)

Fetch live if the analyst wants the primary source; summarize, don't reproduce.

- **Mandiant — "A requirements-driven approach to CTI"** — the philosophy behind starting from decisions, not feeds.
- **Feedly — "How to collect PIRs with stakeholder interviews"** — includes a PIR blueprint and a stakeholder-interview scheduling email template.
- **Red Hat — priority-intelligence-requirements-dev** (GitHub) — a PIR development guide + downloadable template.
- **Intel471 — General Intelligence Requirements (GIR) Handbook** (pts. 1 & 2) — a structured GIR catalog to map your PIRs onto.
- **SEI/CMU — requirements paper** (`2019_011_001_546699.pdf`).
- **CIA — "Words of Estimative Probability"** (pts. 1 & 2) — the estimative-language scale used in `best-practices.md`.
- **MITRE — CTI Blueprints** (GitHub) + SANS 2024 CTI Summit talk — value-based production and report templates.
- **Mandiant — "Better Together: Integrating CTI and Risk Management"** — tying requirements to the risk register.

## How PIR Buddy should cite

When answering a "why / best practice" question, name the source ("per the Definitive Guide's Ch.2…", "Mandiant's requirements-driven approach…") and keep any direct quote short and attributed. Prefer paraphrase. If the analyst wants the exact text, point them to the file/URL rather than pasting it.
